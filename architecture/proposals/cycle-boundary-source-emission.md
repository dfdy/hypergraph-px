# Cycle-boundary source emission — bounded semantic scope

**Accepted behaviour, HPX-OWNER-047; no persistent format or runtime implementation.** The owner affirmatively selects exact original inclusion notation at a stopped cycle, with generated warnings and deliberate navigation controls separate from authored source. [M1](../Conceptual/identity-closure.c4) owns this conceptual refinement; [Pure domain](../C3/pure-domain.c4) owns its pure planning/emission use. [Decision provenance](../../docs/02-governance/OWNER_DECISIONS.md#cycle-boundary-source-notation-and-separate-warnings-2026-10-31) records the explicit coordinator relay rather than claiming independent observation of another chat's original answer.

## Byte ownership and frozen inputs

The notation is a slice of the **including** source at its exact selected version and full occurrence context. It is not copied from the stopped target or the matching ancestor. Target/version/path and ancestor witnesses remain retained metadata even when their handles differ. Retaining notation does not imply expanding that target's body or following a current head.

The pure model consumes the OWNER045 finite selected-state bundle/use tree plus a **frozen source interpretation**. For each exact selected row, that interpretation either supplies an ordered, source-faithful partition or reports missing input. Literal spans and inclusion-notation spans partition the row's exact selected bytes; occurrence/role entries match the row's ordered slots. Expanded children replace only their notation spans; stopped children contribute the exact original notation span. Preserve raw UTF8 bytes and CR/LF/CRLF; there is no normalization or generated placeholder-source branch.

This partition is a mathematical input witness, **not** an adopted notation grammar, parser algorithm, record schema, source-map encoding or new component. The model checks byte bounds, contiguous range partitioning and ordered slot tags. A real adapter must separately establish that the spans actually identify the authored notation and literals, that the selected bytes belong to the authenticated exact source, and that all byte-affecting dependencies are closed. An arbitrary range partition does not prove those premises. Interpretation is a function of the entire selected row under the same frozen source policy; equal policy-ID tokens alone do not prove identical functions or inputs.

Ranges in this model are relative to the selected row's byte list. Mapping them to full immutable-source offsets requires a separately established exact slice relation to the source descriptor's rangeStart/rangeEnd. UTF8 scalar boundaries and browser UTF16 conversion remain in the [anchor/navigation contracts](../../docs/05-domain/text-and-anchors.md); byte-copy proofs do not establish those conversions. Missing source/interpretation, inconsistent partitions or unmatched child slots must fail explicitly, not fabricate notation or silently zip away extra slots. A graph-only inclusion without available original textual notation is not silently assigned a generated or empty textual syntax by this decision.

## Result and source-map boundary

Authored output consists solely of exact owned byte-slice copies with including/selected owner, selected-row reference, full occurrence context and source range. Boundary copies additionally retain the exact stopped target/path/ancestor context. Warning text, accessibility state and navigation controls remain in the separate presentation projection, whose generation cannot alter the authored-byte projection. This separation is semantic, not a chosen wire/storage representation or an edit-authority grant.

The source-map witness must account for ordered copied segments, their output intervals and their original byte ranges without treating notation ownership as ownership of the stopped target. Repeated uses have separate occurrence contexts even when selected source bytes are shared. Empty authored spans may be copied as genuinely supplied input, but do not excuse absent notation evidence. No generic multi-owner editor, historical-range relocation, current permission grant or source/parser correctness follows.
