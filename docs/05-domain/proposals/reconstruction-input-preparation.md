## Proposed admission gates

These are logical checks and outcomes, not a selected transaction protocol or public error schema. Engineering may stage internal work differently if all relationships remain established before a complete result is exposed.

1. **Classify intent and scope.** Distinguish a historical closed read, normal acknowledged-composition reopening, an exact owned-source range and a graph-preserving composition selection. Resolve the intended authorised private context; do not order unrelated users/devices/branches by timestamps or infer ownership from constituent links. Historical reads never follow heads. Normal reopening uses retained acknowledged composition evidence, including working sources; committed fixtures are proof staging only.
2. **Acquire one candidate and establish stability.** Obtain the exact root, selected ordered structure and context-specific selections for that intent. Bind each acquired component to that same candidate. If mutable evidence advances during acquisition, retain the exact established evidence or establish consistency again; do not assemble a state that never existed. The concrete read-view/validation/transaction mechanism remains an engineering dependency. Required recovery evidence must survive constituent draft advancement and uncertain successors under OWNER052, without automatically creating permanent historical captures.
3. **Authenticate and validate full source bindings.** Verify actual owner/source kind/state, explicit supported record version/kind and relevant context-bound envelope/record relationships. Historical immutable revisions and exact retained working material have distinct actual bindings; neither a current draft pointer nor a fabricated RevisionId/SnapshotId can stand for the latter. Validate UTF8 and checked byte ranges against exact full sources; preserve CR/LF/CRLF and Unicode forms. A receipt digest cannot recover missing old bytes or authenticate the semantics of an inclusion.
4. **Validate contribution and selection correspondence.** Establish complete selected structural order, full occurrence paths and current placement; creation origin alone is not current position after a move. Validate both endpoints against the admitted context and exact owned cuts where applicable. Apply OWNER053 membership: coincident byte cuts do not merge distinct structural positions; crossing a zero-byte stop retains its exact selected relationship/context; identical structural endpoints select nothing. Preserve exact partial source bytes and source/occurrence context without relabelling the whole inclusion selected or making clipped notation executable. No implicit expansion or marker-only inclusion discovery.
5. **Establish selected dependency closure.** Resolve every source/structure/interpretation dependency required by that admitted selection, including exact stopped targets and ancestor witnesses, into the finite environment. Repeated shared payloads can still have distinct descendant selections. Prove neither completeness nor access by merely counting slots. Missing/denied/corrupt/unsupported required evidence prevents complete admission; it cannot become empty source, a newer head or a valid cycle boundary. Selection-scoped dependency discovery and authorised disclosure must be designed together; this contract does not require fetching every unrelated graph node or promise success around an inaccessible boundary.
6. **Check the adapter-to-model relation before invoking model guarantees.** Establish the proposed `Admits(E,B,I)` correspondence in the acquisition boundary below: rows map to exact admitted source/state/slices, slots map to complete ordered structural uses, interpretation agrees with source contributions, and contextual selections/ancestors are preserved. Abstract mathematical identity/version tokens need an actual kind-preserving correspondence, not a hash/name coincidence. Existing `PlanOK` does not discharge authentication, syntax fidelity or no-notation/partial-selection correspondence. Do not force graph-only slots into invented zero-length notation.
7. **Expose a complete result only within its evidence and current authority.** Pure traversal/emission may consume admitted inputs only within the model/implementation domain actually established. Preserve exact including-source notation at a stop when present; otherwise invent no authored bytes, retaining relationship/context and separate warnings/navigation. Selection-context metadata is not authored text. Applicable operational limits must hold; exhaustion is explicit non-completeness, not truncation or cycle justification. Check current protected-result authority under the eventual revocation design. This proposed read preparation creates no Commit, branch, permanent reference or cleanup action.

## Acknowledgement, reopening and reconciliation matrix

This matrix derives required distinctions from accepted exact recovery and original-operation semantics; its descriptions are not new wire statuses. [Current Save and receipt code](../../../backend/crates/storage/src/lib.rs) is bounded implementation evidence, not a general acknowledgement protocol.

| Available evidence/condition | Permitted conclusion and necessary handling |
| --- | --- |
| Original operation/body and matching authorised successful receipt; required candidate evidence retained | Original success can be reconciled without a new operation. Independently validate the exact candidate before claiming general composition recovery; the receipt alone supplies no missing source bytes. |
| Request was sent or transport completed, but no valid matching outcome is established | Do not label a new composition acknowledged or discard required predecessor/uncertain-operation evidence. Reconcile the original intent through the actual recovery mechanism. Elapsed time and a new operation ID do not resolve uncertainty. |
| Delayed valid receipt for K arrives after newer local typing or established K+1 | It establishes K's original outcome only. Do not overwrite later typing/selection or roll the current acknowledged state backward by receipt arrival order. Actual established state order must come from the adopted context/operation contract. |
| Same operation ID is used with a changed body, mismatched context or wrong outcome | Conflict/reject that reconciliation attempt; never reinterpret it as success for the changed request. Preserve the recoverable source. |
| Browser lost the pending ID/body, but an authorised current retained acknowledged composition can be independently established | Normal reopening can validate and restore that candidate without claiming the lost request was executed or inventing its body. A read of valid retained state is distinct from proving/retrying a particular write. Do not infer data loss from missing browser intent alone. |
| Browser lost intent and no authoritative acknowledged candidate/result can be established | Report uncertainty/non-completeness for the affected request. Do not fabricate the candidate from current constituent drafts or a last Commit. Persistent intent recovery, if needed, requires its own engineering design; it is not claimed present. |
| Reconciled success exists but its selected working bytes are missing, denied or tied to a different composition | Success of the old operation is not complete current recovery. Return no complete protected reconstruction, preserve evidence and expose only authorised diagnostic detail. |
| Candidate is retained coherently but current authority cannot be established | Retention and a former successful receipt do not grant disclosure. Deny/report uncertainty under the actual permission design without leaking protected source existence. |

A structural selection with identical endpoints has no selected contribution; that does not remove the need to validate its scope/context/authority. A selection crossing a zero-byte stop can instead contain exact structural evidence with empty emitted source. Neither case permits generic permission bypass or reinterpretation of an empty source-range request.

## Future conformance cases and remaining engineering work

These are proposed executable checks to be implemented at the later authorised boundary, not tests run or proofs obtained here:

- **P1:** root bytes from K plus structure/selections from K+1 fails admission even when every record individually decodes; exact K remains recoverable through an uncertain successor.
- **P2:** p and q use the same B source bytes with different nested C selections; admission preserves both contexts and never deduplicates by B identity/version alone.
- **P3:** retained working B changes after K; normal reopen still uses K's exact bytes/context without a cadence-created permanent checkpoint or a substituted base. Existing immutable references and another composition's dependency remain intact.
- **P4:** before-p/after-p selects only p at a shared source cut; a zero-byte stopped p retains exact selected relationship/target/ancestor and separate warnings; identical structural endpoints select nothing.
- **P5:** select only p's emoji inside `A😀B`, or a partial original notation span. Exact bytes and full source/occurrence context survive; q is unaffected; no whole inclusion is invented or range silently expanded. Stale/split-surrogate coordinates fail.
- **P6:** original receipt replay after a lost reply is distinguished from normal reopen with an independently verified candidate after browser intent loss. A successful receipt with missing source bytes cannot satisfy preparation.
- **P7:** corrupt/unsupported binding, missing structural contribution, denied stopped target or changed interpretation prevents complete admission. Original notation remains exact; warning bytes never enter authored source.

The acceptance semantics in P1–P7 are traced to OWNER048–053 and existing source/operation contracts; particular fixtures and test methods remain engineering proposals. No new owner-policy conflict is identified in this increment. The remaining tasks are concrete supplier/read-view and recovery-binding design, supported structural/source recognition with completeness evidence, endpoint-to-source validation, permission integration and an explicit model/Rust correspondence scope. DTOs/codecs, physical source maps, numerical budgets and later application delivery remain separately bounded. No new policy approval is required merely to reject torn, stale, missing or unauthorised input as already specified; implementation details must not be turned into serial owner preferences.

## Proposed acquisition and validation boundary

The following are proposed validation stages, not a chosen wire/record format or executable pipeline. Each stage either supplies evidence for the next or returns a non-complete outcome. “Evidence” here means checked relationships to exact inputs, not a new signed attestation or trusted boolean provided by a caller.

1. **Admit one exact read intent.** The shared application API binds the acting workspace user, workspace, operation, root selection and source-only scope. Resolve selections to exact source-state bindings under the admitted read intent: immutable retained selections for historical reads, or one coherent last-acknowledged working composition for reopening. Working evidence must not be relabelled committed. A first proposed proof/design slice accepts pinned committed selections to bound initial correspondence work;this is not the normal reopening rule or an exclusion of last-autosave nested selections. It does not remove OWNER043/044's intended live capability. Check current authority before protected disclosure; unlock, a node selection or possession of a cached bundle is insufficient.
2. **Obtain authenticated full sources through storage.** Adapters open existing version/kind wrappers and authenticated envelope contexts, validate the claimed logical owner and exact source-state binding plus relevant historical or acknowledged-working relationships, and return exact UTF8 source bytes. The heading includes immutable historical sources and exact captured working inputs; no mutable working pointer alone establishes stability. Wrong kinds/versions/owners/paths and inconsistent publication context fail. Do not reconstruct history from mutable draft text, a current head or a quote search. Same-node parent checks remain distinct from cross-identity derivation; no scanning all ancestry is required merely to prove a slice. Permission/consistent-view semantics for general compositions remain a design dependency, not solved by assuming a SQLite transaction or a decrypted record.
3. **Establish the selected slice.** For each source, validate `0 ≤ start ≤ end ≤ fullByteLength` and UTF8 scalar boundaries against those exact bytes; compute selected bytes directly as `full[start:end]`. Preserve CR/LF/CRLF and composed/decomposed forms. Retain the actual equality to that exact historical or captured working full source, not merely a source length, quote or policy token. A selected-relative span `[lo,hi)` then maps to `[start+lo,start+hi)` with checked bounds/arithmetic. This is precisely the extra relation required by `absolute_copy_index`. Any browser UTF16 input must first be converted against the exact identified owned buffer; this contract does not reuse the browser's LF display projection as source.
4. **Establish ordered inclusion completeness.** Obtain authorial slot order, occurrence/role/exact selected target and known parent/position from the exact historical or coherently acknowledged structural context. Creation must already satisfy OWNER048; a reconstruction read cannot invent missing origin or repair history by guessing. Specify how the adopted representation relates every required structural inclusion to source contribution and output position. Where authored notation exists, establish its exact spans and recognition completeness; where it does not, do not equate absence of syntax with absence of placement or silently omit the inclusion. Check complete ordered correspondence without duplicate/extra/missing slots or contradictory positions. Ordinary references and collection membership are not automatically expansion slots. `PlanOK` checks a supplied literal/notation partition and tags for the current textual model; it does not prove general structural-to-text correspondence or cover alternative representations. Current one-occurrence manifests cannot supply that general relation.
5. **Close a finite selected environment.** Resolve every required target and every source-byte-affecting dependency to exact retained inputs within authorised scope. At stopped cycles retain the exact target descriptor/selection and ancestor context; stopping emission does not license an unverified target. Do not recursively enumerate infinite use paths or allocate a new selected state solely for each path. Apply OWNER045's finite table and structural recurrence semantics after input validation. Repeated uses may share payload bytes but retain their own full root-relative paths and context-dependent descendant selections. Missing dependencies or exhaustion cannot be reclassified as cycle boundaries. Pure planning receives supplied data and has no I/O/permission callback.
6. **Freeze interpretation with inputs.** Bind the exact source/cycle interpretation and any byte-affecting resources to this selected environment. A policy name, mutable configuration or parser version string alone does not establish the same interpretation function. It must either be demonstrably the same supported interpretation over those inputs or report unsupported. Literal/notation ownership is the including source; target and ancestor metadata remain separate. Historical reconstruction cannot silently use the newest parser, profile, provider metadata or selected heads. Physical retention/compatibility for interpretation resources needs its own design before persistence acceptance.
7. **Expose only a validated read result.** Run the bounded models' conceptual construction/emission over validated inputs; do not claim an extracted implementation bridge. Check actual operational work/byte/depth/output limits before or during costly acquisition and emission, including repeated uses; numeric budgets, charging rules and evaluator scheduling remain separately designed. Recheck current authority at the protected result boundary under the eventual revocation/consistency design. If authority or required context cannot be established, return no complete protected result. Warnings/navigation remain presentation metadata outside authored bytes. No draft save, Commit or new closed snapshot is produced by this read-only proposal.

**Accepted:** OWNER049/050 makes last-successfully-acknowledged-autosaved composition the normal reopening boundary. Historical reconstruction instead names a closed historical selection. These are different intents; neither may silently fall back to the other. Both remain subject to current authority and private-state visibility.

**Proposed evidence:** admit an explicitly identified intent and establish the following relationships before calling a result complete. These are logical obligations, not fields in a DTO or a new checkpoint scheme.

An acknowledgement is evidence of its actual operation scope, not proof that the existing one-owned-buffer Save covers general composition. This proposal recommends acknowledging a composition as complete only when its full reconstruction dependency set is recoverable together; it does not require one write per source or an immutable historical version per autosave. How working-source dependencies remain exact after their own drafts change is a required retention/capture design: retained exact evidence or another approved recovery mechanism is needed, not a moving pointer. If such evidence is absent, the design cannot promise successful reopening. No cadence retention, deletion or publication policy is adopted. Earlier ADR019 and OWNER010 scope remains as recorded; universal future autosave/Commit separation is unresolved.

For a delayed or lost reply, reconcile the original operation and its frozen composition through authorised recovery before choosing a candidate. Do not call later unacknowledged typing durable or let a stale reply replace the currently established acknowledged state. Ordering and recovery must use the adopted operation/acknowledgement semantics, not a new sequence algorithm inferred here. A separately authorised newer-version query may decorate the reopened result; it never participates in determining its bytes or selections. Save-as-branch consumes an exact validated constructed composition under the same logical identity, but capture/publication is a separate future write design.

### Structural position to source projection: evidence that is actually needed

OWNER048 establishes known creation origin/parent-or-position, not a permanent containment parent, a textual coordinate or a reconstruction order by itself. For each selected structural context, obtain evidence of the current ordered placement relationships under that exact composition. A creation origin cannot be reused as the current position after a move without evidence. Source-owner identity, placement identity and full root-relative occurrence context remain separate.

Recommend an **ordered contribution relation** as the next semantic design boundary. It relates each structural inclusion use to its selected target and position among exact owned source runs. Each source run names exact bytes/range; each inclusion contribution names its structural occurrence/context and target selection. When notation exists, the relation also identifies its original including-source span. This is a proposed mathematical relationship, not a persisted list, source-map encoding, parser grammar or universal textual insertion rule.

Evidence must establish all of the following:

1. **Structural coverage:** enumerate the required selected inclusions completely from authoritative structure, with stable occurrence/context and role. Identify references and collection membership separately; neither becomes an expansion slot merely by being an edge. Missing structural records do not mean an empty child list.
2. **Placement/order fidelity:** relate every required inclusion exactly once to the selected parent's contribution order, preserving repeated and nested use context. If several inclusions share a textual cut, authoritative structural order must distinguish them; no sort by ID/name/time is inferred. Known parent without position/order evidence is insufficient for ordered text.
3. **Source fidelity:** every copied run is an exact owned slice; notation spans, where present, belong to the including source and correspond to actual recognised notation under the frozen interpretation. Recognising a marker requires a specified interpretation with soundness/completeness evidence; bounds and matching slot counts alone cannot supply it. Repeated text or an apparent marker is never a placement locator.
4. **Projection completeness:** account explicitly for every inclusion's contribution, including placements with no textual notation. Cover intended owned source runs without accidental loss/duplication, establish any source-versus-output overlap rules, and distinguish zero-byte source contribution from absent structural evidence. A complete structural graph and valid byte ranges do not alone prove this relation.
5. **Boundary correspondence:** when a cycle stops at an inclusion with original notation, copy those exact including-source bytes under OWNER047, retaining target and ancestor separately. Where no original notation exists, the current textual model supplies no adopted source contribution rule. Do not generate notation, omit the structural inclusion or label an unresolved correspondence proved.
6. **Selection correspondence:** for selected subranges, specify how structural contributions belong to that selection, including an empty source, placements at endpoints and ranges crossing a notation/projection boundary. Do not infer inclusion/exclusion from a byte cut or adopt marker clipping rules. Existing exact single-source historical slice reads keep their scope.

These obligations separate trustworthy acquisition from checklist item 3's physical closure/source-map design. A future parser may prove only the notation part; an authoritative structural reader must also establish graph-only coverage. A caller's supplied relation can be a candidate, never its own completeness certificate. Current `Manifest`/`Snapshot` readers validate one owned occurrence; they do not implement this enumeration/projection evidence.

### Proposed adapter-to-pure-model relation

Let E denote the exact admitted evidence for one read intent, B a finite selected-state bundle, and I a frozen source interpretation. Propose a relation `Admits(E, B, I)`, with no implementation or theorem claimed:

- Authority and successful acknowledgement/closed-history selection are checked outside pure planning. B contains only the permitted supplied data; the relation never turns a mathematical token into a grant. Current-authority result disclosure still needs the eventual revocation/consistent-read mechanism.
- Source correspondence maps each B row to the exact full owned source and selected slice in E, preserving kind, identity, actual source-state binding, bytes, ranges, byte-affecting dependencies and descendant selections. Working evidence is not relabelled an immutable revision. Equal aliases must agree in all byte/selection-affecting context; distinct contexts are not collapsed merely because text matches.
- Structural correspondence maps each row's ordered slots to the complete required structural inclusions in E. Targets resolve into the finite total table, and use paths/ancestor stacks are preserved independently of payload sharing. Closure discovery and current-authority acquisition happen before pure traversal; no I/O/permission callbacks enter the domain.
- Emission correspondence relates I's supplied plan to the actual ordered contribution relation. For the current textual model, establish `PlanOK` and source faithfulness in addition to bounds/tag agreement. Original-notation spans retain their including owner and exact absolute-source offset. A graph-only contribution cannot be encoded as invented zero-length notation just to satisfy `PlanOK`; a justified model extension or explicit correspondence proof is needed.
- Stability binds the admitted candidate and interpretation to one exact composition cut, never a mixture of moving reads. Pure traversal can then establish only its conditional finite-use results; byte emission can claim its checked results only for inputs within that model's stated domain.

An eventual bridge theorem should state these premises and show preservation of exact selections/use coverage and owned emitted bytes. Adapter checks, decoder/authentication correctness, structural-reader/parser fidelity, UTF8/browser conversion and Rust execution must each have implementation evidence; a mathematical witness or more synthetic trusted rows cannot discharge them. No Lean source or assumptions change in this proposal, so no proof replay or extracted-Rust claim follows.

### Coherent acquisition and uncertain acknowledgements

The following consequences follow from the accepted exact-recovery/authority/no-substitution obligations; the candidate preparation protocol that realises them remains **proposed**. No particular transaction, lock, wire token, sequence allocator or persistent record is selected.

1. **Choose the read intent first.** A historical closed read resolves its exact immutable sources and frozen selections. Normal reopen selects the last successfully acknowledged composition in its authorised private editing context, including exact working sources. Neither substitutes the last Commit or current constituent drafts. Different private editing contexts are not ordered by clock time or receipt arrival.
2. **Freeze a composition candidate before acknowledgement.** Identify exact root-owned bytes, ordered structure/current placement, full occurrence-context selections, cycle targets and byte-affecting dependencies/interpretation. Distinguish selected working evidence from committed revisions and preserve context-specific descendants. Required inputs must be recoverable together before claiming a durable complete acknowledgement. This need not mean one write per source or a new immutable object per autosave.
3. **Validate all acquired relationships against that same candidate.** A source can be valid by itself yet belong to the wrong candidate. Authenticate each actual source-state binding and selected range; verify structural completeness and OWNER051 contribution correspondence; establish that mutable evidence did not change between reads or that an exact retained copy is being used. Detect a torn root/structure/selection set and retry or fail explicitly rather than label it complete. The finite selected environment covers stopped-cycle targets too; missing/denied targets remain missing/denied, not cycle stops.
4. **Separate operation success, received acknowledgement and recovery.** A sent request or elapsed time proves none of them. A valid delayed reply establishes only its original captured operation; it never overwrites newer browser typing or turns an old candidate into the latest acknowledged state. Reconcile an uncertain request using its original identity/body and authorised result evidence under the eventual operation contract. If the original intent cannot be recovered or matched, report that uncertainty instead of creating a fresh supposedly equivalent request. Do not infer an existing generic browser-restart intent journal. Reconciliation may establish success and acknowledge it now; this proposal does not define every cross-context meaning of “last” or equate server completion with a reply previously received by the browser.
5. **Retain a recoverable acknowledged state through an uncertain successor.** Beginning K+1 must not make K unrecoverable while the successor's durability/success remains unresolved. Once authorised reconciliation establishes a recoverable successor, reopening can use that established state. Exact handling of competing private contexts, operation recovery after restart and failure atomicity must be designed and checked; no new last-writer-wins rule follows. No generic multi-owner whole-buffer write is authorised.
6. **Disclose under current authority.** Retaining bytes is not authority to read them later, share private state, or reveal inaccessible source existence. Acquisition and protected result disclosure need the actual permissions/revocation contract. An inaccessible retained dependency yields no complete reconstruction. A newer-version query is separate and cannot retarget the admitted candidate.

### Structural selection and endpoint correspondence

Accepted text anchors already bind exact owner/source/range and checked UTF16 conversion, while OWNER051 permits structure without notation and zero authored bytes at a stopped cycle. Consequently a parent-source byte offset, or a flattened output byte offset alone, cannot determine general structural membership. OWNER053 now accepts the selection meanings in this proposal, while endpoint representations and physical mechanisms remain unadopted. This is not an adopted source-map format, grammar or restriction on arbitrary owned-source selection.

- **Keep the intent explicit.** An owned-source range asks for exactly that owner's bytes under its existing contract. A composition selection asks for an ordered portion of the admitted finite contribution/use context, with structural membership as well as bytes. Do not route the latter to an owned-source slice and silently drop inclusions. A text anchor is not automatically an inclusion or a newly created node.
- **Use structurally distinguishable endpoints.** Recommend endpoints that identify the exact composition/context and a boundary before/after a selected contribution, or a checked cut within an exact owned source run at a full occurrence path. Byte conversion is a checked part of locating an interior source cut; it does not supply missing structural affinity. Endpoints inside a child retain that child's full context, so repeated equal text is unambiguous. No persistent endpoint schema or ID representation is selected.
- **Define membership by contribution order.** Propose a half-open interval in that structural order. Include source slices intersecting the interval and structural inclusion/stop contributions lying between its endpoints. A zero-byte stop still has distinct before/after positions and its exact relationship/target/ancestor; it cannot disappear because source-byte intersection is empty. An empty interval at one endpoint selects nothing; an interval crossing a structural zero-byte contribution can select it with empty emitted source. Partial child selection retains selected slices and ancestry/context without claiming the whole child was selected.
- **Preserve the role of original notation.** A cut inside original notation can identify an exact including-owner byte fragment under the source-range contract; that fragment is not an independently valid inclusion token. Do not infer selected structural relationships by reparsing clipped text or forbid arbitrary source slices merely because they clip a marker. A composition selection must separately carry/check its intended relationship membership and fragment context before claiming reconstructible structural content. No notation grammar or universal clipping policy is adopted here.
- **Reject ambiguous mappings, not valid graph-only content.** A request containing only coincident byte offsets is insufficient to choose structural membership. Obtain explicit before/after/context intent or return ambiguity; do not guess by ID, text, nearest marker or endpoint bias. Required acquisition/disclosure depends on actual selection scope and permissions; this does not promise a partial selection succeeds around an inaccessible adjacent source or expose its existence.

| Semantic example — accepted meaning under OWNER053; not an executed test | Composition/context | Proposed outcome |
| --- | --- | --- |
| S1 — repeated placements at one parent-source cut | Exact contribution order is parent-owned `L`, p→B, q→C, parent-owned `R`; both placements have parent-source byte cut1. | `[before p, after p)` selects p, not q; `[before q, after q)` selects q. Parent-source range offsets cannot distinguish these cases. |
| S2 — zero-byte cycle stop | p is a no-notation stop under OWNER051; before/after p have the same flattened source-byte offset. | Crossing p retains its stopped relationship/context and separate warning/navigation even though its source contribution is empty. `[before p, before p)` is empty. | S3 — partial nested child | p→B's owned source contains `A😀B`, with another B occurrence q. | Interior checked cuts around p's emoji select its exact source bytes/context, not q or all of B. | S4 — changed or stale context | Same visible text now has reordered p/q, a newer draft generation or a different stopped ancestor. | The old selection remains bound to its admitted state or is rejected/remapped explicitly; current text equality cannot retarget it. |
| S5 — original notation fragment | User selects only part of the exact original including-source notation span. | Preserve the exact owned byte fragment; do not invent a complete token or silently infer/rewrite its target relationship. This proposal addresses selection meaning only. Physical closure/source-map design, operational limits, conflict-state policy and implementation correspondence remain checklist items2–5, not work advanced here.
