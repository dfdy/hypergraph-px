## Principle statement

Autonomy, intellectual agency and writing-first use constrain later engineering. Authorship, influence, adoption, endorsement, verification and responsibility are separate propositions with their own evidence. The [purpose](project-purpose.md) is the source of these commitments; [provenance](../05-domain/provenance-model.md) supplies exact semantics. Do not silently turn philosophical rationale into machine-verifiable claims. Privacy is not defeated by comprehensive provenance capture; disclosure is scoped.
